# Audio audit: the "Made for Feetza" sounds

Every sound credited as "Made for Feetza" in `public/audio/credits.json` (synthesized with ffmpeg and Python, often layered with Kenney CC0 impacts) as of the start of the Freesound audio pass: 517 files. Each has what it is (from its credit and where the code plays it), how often a player hears it, and a verdict:

- **replace**: a real recording would clearly be better (the kind is named);
- **layer**: keep the synth, add a real layer;
- **keep**: stylised (magic, sci-fi, UI, music) that a recording would not improve.

Priorities for replace and layer: **P1** heard constantly or in Acts 1–3 (smacks, thrown props, the early bosses) and clearly weak; **P2** boss moves and the mid and late acts, and hero and god smack tails; **P3** rare (hero and god peel moves, nemesis stages, sightings). **done** means it was replaced in batch 1 (see below).

Sounds are built with `scripts/real-sfx/build.py` from Freesound HQ previews fetched with `scripts/sfx-fetch/freesound.mjs` (CC0 and CC-BY only), replacing the file under the same name; every source is credited in `public/audio/CREDITS.md` and `public/audio/credits.json`.
